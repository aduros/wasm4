# Journey to the Steam Planet

You play as a steam rocket on a journey to the steam planet.
Shoot asteroids and UFOs, avoid deadly steam puffs, and collect fuel to advance
to the next level. But be careful, as you can't use your gun while you're
charging up your engine!

Sadly the titular steam planet is too far away to be reached by the rocket, but
that won't stop you from trying to get as far as you can!

Made for the [Zero Sugar Added Jam](https://ziggit.dev/t/zero-sugar-added-first-edition/16795).
