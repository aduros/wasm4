# Cosmic Hopper

Zorp, a little alien, fell out of his UFO and landed in the middle of a cosmic
highway. Guide him across a lane of speeding spaceships and a river of stardust
to reach the rescue landing pads on the other side — before his lives run out!
Watchout not to fall into the void of space, or worse, getting run over by a 
space vehicle!

## How to play

- Use **⬅ ➡ ⬆ ⬇** to hop Zorp one tile at a time.
- Avoid the spaceships crossing the highway.
- Ride the drifting asteroid platforms to cross the stardust river — don't fall
  into the void between them!
- Guide Zorp into each of the 4 empty landing pads to fill them all and clear
  the stage.
- Clearing a stage grants a bonus life and raises the difficulty for the next
  one.

## Stages

1. **Stage 1** — slow traffic and wide, forgiving platforms. A gentle warm-up.
2. **Stage 2** — normal speed and spacing.
3. **Stage 3** — fast lanes and narrow platforms. Only a steady hand
   gets Zorp home!

Clear all 3 stages to send Zorp back to his UFO and win the game.
